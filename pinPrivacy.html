Information Collection and Use

The Application is an educational Chinese Pinyin vocabulary game designed for students, classrooms, and learners of all ages.

  • Microphone & Audio Processing: The Application uses your device's microphone solely for real-time speech recognition during gameplay (shouting Chinese Pinyin words to execute attacks). Speech processing is handled on-device using Android's built-in speech recognition framework. The Service Provider does not record, store, transmit, or share your voice recordings or audio data.
  • Game Progress Data: Game scores, team configurations, and custom vocabulary settings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to external cloud databases.
  • Personal Data: We do not collect, sell, or share personal identifiable information (such as your name, address, or contacts).
